The Home

Historic Craftsman character, thoughtfully updated for today

Walk through the original Craftsman door and the first thing you notice is the beautiful light and the breathtaking oak millwork.

The home was completely rebuilt in 2002 while retaining the original 1920 Craftsman architectural details. The dining room still has its 1920 built-in buffet and beautiful glass doors.

Everything else is all ready for modern daily life. A granite kitchen with an island and a skylight. A family room with a gas fireplace. A separate office and library lined with cabinetry. Spacious bedrooms with walk-in closets. Central air, a central vacuum, an alarm system and owned solar.

You experience the beauty of a historic Craftsman with none of the worries that come with a 100-year-old home.

And there's another surprise: above the garage is a separate retreat of ~600 square feet, with its own entrance, a full bath, a walk-in closet and two balconies. It is ready to be spacious guest quarters, a private studio or a quiet place to meet clients.
